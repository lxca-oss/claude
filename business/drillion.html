Pick a shaft

Each key business function has its own shaft and its own rooms, and the Deep Drill mixes all four. The Daily Dig is the same 7 prompts for everyone today, so you can compare depths with your class. Endless keeps going until you lose three hard hats.

How a dig works

  1. Read the categoryPrompts are open categories from your flashcards, like “Name a source of short-term borrowing”.
  2. Type one answerAny valid answer scores. Obvious ones score 10. Sharp, specific ones score up to 100. A related answer that doesn't quite fit still earns a pebble.
  3. Learn from the revealAfter each prompt you see every valid answer, shallow to deep, with a one-line explanation. Then the drill moves you down.
